Write a literature review on: $ARGUMENTS

Follow the scholarly-research skill:
1. Run at least 3 `list_articles` searches with different keyword sets (core topic, methods or mechanisms, population or context; add Indonesian keywords if the topic is Indonesia-related). Use a limit of 8-10.
2. Organize the review by theme: background, main findings, points of disagreement, gaps.
3. Cite every claim to a retrieved article (author/title, year, DOI). Only describe findings the results actually support.
4. End with a references list in the requested style (default APA 7) and a short "limitations of this search" note.
